# NovaMart Retail Performance Analysis: Executive Brief

**Prepared for:** Executive Leadership Team (CFO, VP of Sales, Head of Merchandising)  
**Period Covered:** January 1, 2023 – December 31, 2024 (24 Months)  
**Dataset Scope:** 1,200 Orders, 2,060 Order Items, 250 Customers, 40 Products  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Over the 2023–2024 operating period, NovaMart achieved:
- **Total Revenue (Completed Orders):** \$174,869.31
- **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):** \$71,543.40
- **Gross Profit:** \$103,325.91
- **Overall Gross Profit Margin:** 59.09%
- **Completed Orders:** 1,092 (91.0% completion rate)
- **Active Customers:** 245
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** \$160.14

While aggregate revenue shows sustained momentum and healthy baseline margins (59.1%), granular analysis reveals critical strategic vulnerabilities:
1. **The Volume vs. Margin Trade-off:** High-volume Electronics generates 42.1% of revenue (\$73.6k) but produces the lowest margin (50.85%). Higher-margin categories like Beauty & Health (71.45% margin) and Apparel (67.59% margin) are significantly under-indexed in sales volume.
2. **Margin Erosion Through Discounting:** Orders with 15% discount saw gross margins plummet to 52.13%—an erosion of 9.38 percentage points compared to undiscounted sales (61.51%).
3. **Outsized Reliance on Repeat Customers:** 86.9% of customers are repeat buyers, driving 96.39% (\$168.6k) of company revenue.
4. **Geographic Variance:** The South leads with an AOV of \$172.59, while the West underperforms at \$147.59 AOV and lowest total revenue (\$36.3k).

## 3. Strategic Recommendations for Leadership

1. **Strategic Product Bundling (Cross-Category Attach):**
   - *Problem:* Electronics drives 42% of revenue but drags blended margin down (50.85%).
   - *Action:* Create bundles pairing low-margin tech purchases with high-margin items (e.g., Laptop stand + Tech accessories pouch from Apparel, or Monitor + Blue Light Skincare Mist).
2. **Restructure Discount Policy:**
   - *Problem:* 15% discount drops margin from 61.5% to 52.1%.
   - *Action:* Replace flat markdown coupons with order-threshold incentives (e.g., "Spend \$180, get \$15 off" or "Free Express Shipping on orders over \$150"). This protects gross margin while lifting AOV.
3. **Nurture Small Business B2B Accounts:**
   - *Problem:* Small Business is the smallest customer count (50), but highest revenue per account (\$749.53).
   - *Action:* Launch a dedicated Small Business VIP Program offering quarterly bulk discounts, business invoicing, and dedicated customer support to drive larger basket sizes.
4. **Targeted Second-Purchase Re-engagement:**
   - *Problem:* One-time buyers generate only \$197 lifetime spend before dropping off.
   - *Action:* Deploy an automated email sequence on Day 14 and Day 30 post-delivery offering category-specific replenishment recommendations to convert single buyers into repeat buyers.
5. **Optimize West Coast Logistics & Merchandising:**
   - *Problem:* West Coast lags in revenue (\$36.3k) and AOV (\$147.59).
   - *Action:* Review transit times and shipping carriers for West Coast shipments; run localized seasonal promotions aligned with Pacific lifestyle and apparel preferences.
